# Fortress of the Muslim (Hisnul Muslim)

**Author:** Said bin Ali Al-Qahtani  
**Language:** English  
**Category:** Islamic Supplications / Dua  
**Source:** archive.org (Public Domain)

## Description

*Fortress of the Muslim* (Arabic: حصن المسلم, Hisnul Muslim) is one of the most widely read Islamic books on supplications and remembrances. It is a compact and comprehensive collection of authentic du'as (supplications) and adhkar (remembrances) derived from the Quran and the Sunnah of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ).

The book covers supplications for virtually every occasion in a Muslim's daily life — from waking up in the morning to going to sleep at night, from entering the mosque to traveling, from times of happiness to times of distress.

## Key Topics

- Morning and evening supplications
- Supplications for daily activities
- Prayers for protection from harm
- Remembrance of Allah in various situations
- Supplications during illness and hardship

## Why This Book Matters in Comparative Religion

This book offers a window into Islamic spirituality and the intimate relationship Muslims maintain with God through constant remembrance and supplication. It demonstrates the role of prayer and devotion in Islam as a complete way of life.
