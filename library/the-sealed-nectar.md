# The Sealed Nectar (Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum)

**Author:** Sheikh Safi-ur-Rahman Al-Mubarakpuri  
**Language:** English  
**Category:** Islamic Biography (Seerah)  
**Source:** archive.org (Public Domain)

## Description

*The Sealed Nectar* (Arabic: الرحيق المختوم, Ar-Raheeq Al-Makhtum) is an award-winning biography of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ). It won first prize in a worldwide competition on the biography of the Prophet held by the Muslim World League in Makkah in 1979.

This comprehensive Seerah (biography) covers the life of the Prophet from his birth to his death, tracing the history of early Islam, its struggles, triumphs, and the transformation of Arabian society.

## Key Topics

- Birth and early life of Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ)
- The beginning of revelation
- The migration (Hijra) to Madinah
- Major battles and events of early Islam
- The character and personality of the Prophet
- The final years and passing of the Prophet

## Why This Book Matters in Comparative Religion

A detailed, scholarly biography of Islam's founder provides essential context for understanding Islamic beliefs, practices, and history. Comparing prophetic biographies across religions offers rich insight into how religions understand divine messengers and their missions.
