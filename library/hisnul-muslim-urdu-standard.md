# Hisnul Muslim Urdu (Standard Text Edition)

**Author:** Said bin Ali Al-Qahtani (Urdu translation)  
**Language:** Urdu  
**Category:** Islamic Supplications / Dua  
**Source:** archive.org (Public Domain)

## Description

This is the standard text Urdu edition of *Hisnul Muslim* (Fortress of the Muslim — حصن المسلم), one of the most popular Islamic books of supplications in the world. This particular edition is presented in clear Urdu text, making it accessible to the millions of Urdu-speaking Muslims across Pakistan, India, and the diaspora.

The book remains a staple in Muslim households, widely gifted at weddings, graduations, and Islamic occasions as a practical companion for daily spiritual life.

## Key Topics

- Complete collection of Islamic du'as in Urdu
- Supplications organized by life situation
- Easy-to-read Urdu text with Arabic originals
- Comprehensive coverage of daily Islamic practice
- Accessible format for general readers

## Why This Book Matters in Comparative Religion

The widespread popularity of devotional prayer books across all major religions — from Christian prayer books, to Jewish siddurs, to Islamic du'a collections — reveals a universal human need for structured communication with the divine. Comparing these traditions illuminates both shared spiritual instincts and distinct theological approaches to prayer.
