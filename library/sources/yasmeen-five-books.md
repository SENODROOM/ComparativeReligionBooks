## Relevance

- **Endless Bliss, First Fascicle:** a Hanafi/Sunni presentation of Islamic belief and spiritual practice.
- **Endless Bliss, Fourth Fascicle:** a complementary volume on Hanafi worship and religious practice; a distinct volume from the first fascicle.
- **Islâm:** Ameer Ali's historical explanation and defense of Islamic teachings and ethics.
- **A Critical Exposition of the Popular 'Jihád':** Cherágh Ali's historical and theological argument concerning defensive warfare and compulsory conversion. Included as historical scholarship, not an endorsement of every historical claim.
- **The Confessions of Al Ghazzali:** a classical account of the search for religious knowledge, philosophy and Sufism, in Claud Field's English translation.

## Rights and attribution

The publisher's note on PDF page 2 of each Endless Bliss volume grants advance permission to reproduce the book in its original form, subject to the stated accuracy and production conditions. Both supplied files are byte-for-byte copies of the official downloads, preserving that note and the original publisher attribution. They are not labeled public domain or Creative Commons.

Each Project Gutenberg catalog page lists its edition as public domain in the USA. The complete source text, attribution, credits and Project Gutenberg license remain inside each converted PDF. The PDFs are noncommercial format conversions, with line wrapping and pagination changed but no source text omitted. Original page-number references in the source text refer to the source edition, not the conversion's new PDF pagination. No claim is made that these are facsimile scans. Rights outside the USA should be assessed under the relevant jurisdiction.
