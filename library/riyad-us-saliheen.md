# Riyad Us Saliheen (Gardens of the Righteous)

**Author:** Imam Yahya ibn Sharaf Al-Nawawi  
**Language:** Arabic  
**Category:** Islamic Hadith Collection  
**Source:** archive.org (Public Domain)

## Description

*Riyad Us Saliheen* (Arabic: رياض الصالحين, "Gardens of the Righteous") is one of the most celebrated and widely read collections of Hadith (sayings and actions of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ). Compiled by the 13th-century Islamic scholar Imam Al-Nawawi, it contains approximately 1900 hadiths organized by theme.

The book is beloved by Muslims worldwide for its emphasis on ethics, character, spiritual refinement, and practical guidance for everyday Muslim life.

## Key Topics

- Sincerity and intention in worship
- Patience, gratitude, and trust in God
- Rights of family and neighbors
- Honesty, justice, and good character
- Knowledge and its importance
- Love, brotherhood, and community

## Why This Book Matters in Comparative Religion

The ethical teachings compiled in Riyad Us Saliheen share deep parallels with moral frameworks in other Abrahamic religions. Comparative study of these hadiths alongside Biblical Proverbs or Jewish ethical literature reveals both commonalities and distinctions in religious moral traditions.
